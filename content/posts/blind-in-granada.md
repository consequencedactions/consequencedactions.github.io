“não há pior desgraça que cego em Granada.” 
 
My grandfather 
hands me the sentence 
like it's older than both of us. 
 
Blind. 
 
Funny how the word 
fits over so many eyes. 
 
The ones that read 
without looking. 
 
The ones that look 
without seeing. 
 
I used to think a poem 
had to grow. 
 
Now I think 
it has to erode. 
 
Chip away 
at the noise 
 
until what’s left 
could have been 
hidden inside the stone 
all along.
