Books and women share a spine. 
 
One is judged by how well it holds together. 
The other by how long it can. 
 
We call them 
well-read 
well-bread 
well-bound,   
 
ironic how every compliment sounds like a way to keep something  closed. 
 
A bent page 
becomes a bookmark. 
A bent woman 
becomes a warning. 
 
I wonder 
how many of us mistook 
breaking 
for opening. 
 
Or whether we were simply taught, 
that a cracked spine is easier to forgive on a book, 
than on a woman.
